# How the icon was chosen

The proposals behind [the Arbalete icon](../icon.svg), kept to show how it was chosen. They are not used by the
app. All of them are original drawings: the brief was Arc's playful spirit, without its looping-A mark, around
the idea of a crossbow ("arbalète") bolt.

## 1. Concepts

[1-concepts](1-concepts): five directions, each as an app icon, a bare mark and a single-color version.

1. **Arrow A**: the letter A crossed by a fletched bolt, which becomes its crossbar.
2. **Loop Bolt**: one stroke that loops once, then flies off as an arrow.
3. **Crossbow**: a crossbow seen from above, aimed up and to the right.
4. **Bullseye**: a bolt landing in a target.
5. **Pointe**: a hollow arrowhead whose outline forms an A.

Kept: 1 and 5, then 3, for being clearly an A.

## 2. Rounder

[2-rounder](2-rounder) ([sheet](2-rounder/sheet.png)): three rounder variations of 1, 3 and 5, with thicker
strokes, round caps and no sharp tips; the crossbow ones were redrawn so that the bow forms the A.

Kept: **1b Chunky**, the original A made chunkier, the sturdiest at 16 px.

## 3. Colors

[3-colors](3-colors) ([sheet](3-colors/sheet.png)): Chunky in six palettes inspired by Arc's vivid colors:
spectrum on light and dark, red to blue, pastel, one color per stroke, and amber on night.

Kept: **Spectrum, dark**, a red-to-blue spectrum mark on a dark rounded square.

## 4. Tail

Seen in the Dock next to Arc's icon, the mark needed more room, a white outline to stand out, and better
centering. Its V-shaped fletching then read as two round lobes, so [4-tails](4-tails)
([sheet](4-tails/sheet.png)) explored six tails: ribbed feathers, a longer tail, three vanes, leaf feathers, a
rising bolt and a swallow tail.

Kept: the **swallow tail** on the **rising bolt**, raised slightly so the bolt crosses the A higher
([1-previous](5-open-a/1-previous.svg)).

## 5. An open A

[5-open-a](5-open-a) ([sheet](5-open-a/sheet.png)): the mark needed more weight and a visible hole in the middle of
the A. Thicker strokes ([2-bolder](5-open-a/2-bolder.svg)) closed the narrow A's counter, and cutting a hole into
it ([3-cut-counter](5-open-a/3-cut-counter.svg)) thinned the letter around it. So the A was redrawn wider and
taller, with a real counter outlined like the rest of the mark, and a longer bolt that crosses the whole letter;
two tops were tried, pointed and [round](5-open-a/4-open-round-top.svg).

Kept: the **pointed top**. That is the final [icon.svg](../icon.svg), with [icon-mono.svg](../icon-mono.svg) for
single-color use.
